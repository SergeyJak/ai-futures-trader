# SOL 12h regime cohort study (frozen 2026-11-10)

This is a read-only analysis of corrected, closed paper trades, not a new live strategy. It compares dip continuation, dip reversal and momentum continuation on 100 USDT starting capital and 10 USDT fixed entry notional. The original 2026-10-30 holdout remains descriptive for these newly reported hypotheses. The new prospective cutoff is **2026-11-11 00:00 UTC**. Trades crossing either cutoff are purged. No thresholds are selected from the prospective sample.

Primary prospective hypothesis (selected on the now-descriptive earlier holdout): **dip continuation 12h SHORT-only**. The four secondary filters are exploratory and cannot be promoted from this run. Frozen secondary filters: direction-aligned 60m return >= 0.3%, realized 60m volatility <= 1.2%, 15m/45m volume ratio >= 1.2%, and their intersection. Report LONG/SHORT, up/down/range, volatility and paired filtered-minus-baseline results on identical opportunities (skips = zero). This is a conditional cohort, not a replay of additional signals freed by skipped positions. The single primary decision is frozen at the first UTC day on which at least 30 accepted trades and 20 active days have accrued, after that day plus 12h has elapsed. Later outcomes never move this checkpoint. Require positive net and stress net, positive paired delta and positive lower 95% two-day block-bootstrap bounds before labeling positive paper evidence. Ongoing metrics before/after the checkpoint are descriptive, not repeated significance tests. No claim of execution proof.

Use corrected next-open-v2 prices, embedded slippage, both fees, public funding rates with preceding confirmed minute close as mark-notional proxy, and adverse treatment of ambiguous boundary settlements. Stress adds 3 bps per side. Funding cash flows and account fee tiers remain unverified. Missing funding coverage is not zero. Report closed and minute-close mark-to-market drawdown (not intrabar extrema). Deduplicate, reject overlapping positions causally, require complete confirmed entry and position candles, and fail closed on truncated prospective history. The existing report's 150-row limit permits a complete prospective sample only while the oldest returned entry predates the new cutoff; older development/holdout metrics are explicitly marked truncated. The artifact retains per-trade prices, regime, modeled costs and settlement details for audit.

Run: python3 -m unittest discover -s research/dip-regime-forward -p 'test_*.py'; python3 research/dip-regime-forward/study.py --output dip-regime-report.json.
