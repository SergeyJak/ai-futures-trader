# SOL 12h regime cohort study (frozen 2026-11-10)

This is a read-only analysis of corrected, closed paper trades, not a new live strategy. It compares dip continuation, dip reversal and momentum continuation on 100 USDT starting capital and 10 USDT fixed entry notional. The original 2026-10-30 holdout remains descriptive for these newly reported hypotheses. The new prospective cutoff is **2026-11-11 00:00 UTC**. Trades crossing either cutoff are purged. No thresholds are selected from the prospective sample.

Frozen filters: direction-aligned 60m return >= 0.3%, realized 60m volatility <= 1.2%, 15m/45m volume ratio >= 1.2%, and their intersection. Report LONG/SHORT, up/down/range, volatility and paired filtered-minus-baseline results on identical opportunities (skips = zero). This is a conditional cohort, not a replay of additional signals freed by skipped positions. Require 30 accepted trades, 20 active days, positive net and stress net, positive paired delta and positive lower 95% two-day block-bootstrap bounds before labeling positive paper evidence. No claim of execution proof.

Use corrected next-open-v2 prices, embedded slippage, both fees, public funding rates with preceding confirmed minute close as mark-notional proxy, and adverse treatment of ambiguous boundary settlements. Stress adds 3 bps per side. Funding cash flows and account fee tiers remain unverified. Missing funding coverage is not zero. Report closed and minute-close mark-to-market drawdown (not intrabar extrema). Deduplicate, reject overlapping positions causally, require complete confirmed entry and position candles, and fail closed on truncated trade history. The existing report's 150-row limit is an explicit future capacity constraint.

Run: python3 -m unittest discover -s research/dip-regime-forward -p 'test_*.py'; python3 research/dip-regime-forward/study.py --output dip-regime-report.json. The existing research archive workflow runs the study as an independent job; no Railway settings, order logic, or paper balances are changed.
